# Reusable Lesson Components

These components define the required structure for curriculum content. A lesson may omit a component only when it is genuinely irrelevant.

## Lesson Snapshot

- Curriculum and lesson number
- Title
- Primary Scripture
- Supporting Scripture
- Big idea
- Main theme
- Memory verse
- Audience and difficulty
- Suggested 45-, 60-, and 90-minute plans

## Leader Preparation

- Prayer prompt
- Personal reflection before teaching
- Background reading
- Sensitive issues or safeguarding concerns

## Biblical Background

- Literary context
- Historical and cultural setting
- Geography, people, customs, and timeline when helpful
- Why the background changes or clarifies interpretation

## Passage Walkthrough

Divide the primary text into natural sections. For each section include:

- What happens
- What it means
- What it reveals about God or Jesus
- What it reveals about people
- A teaching or discussion prompt

## Theology

- Major doctrines in the passage
- Clear definitions
- Connections to the gospel
- Boundaries between established teaching and disputed interpretation

## Scripture Connections

- Old Testament connections
- New Testament connections
- Explanation of why each connection matters

## Biblical Worldview

Answer how Scripture shapes Christian thinking about the lesson’s subject. Avoid reducing biblical worldview to political alignment.

## Current Culture

- Describe the present issue accurately and fairly
- Identify opportunities, dangers, assumptions, and pressures
- Avoid sensationalism and quickly outdated claims

## Difficult Questions

For each question provide:

- A concise response
- Biblical principles
- What is known and unknown
- A follow-up question that encourages thoughtful discussion

## Discussion Coaching

- Observation, interpretation, application, and mission questions
- Follow-up prompts if discussion stalls
- Guidance for disagreement or strong emotions
- Ways to return the group to Scripture

## Teaching Outline

- Introduction
- Three or fewer main movements when possible
- Transitions
- Illustration or example
- Application
- Conclusion

## Practical Ministry

Consider personal life, family, church, work, community, mentoring, and mission. Select only the pathways relevant to the lesson.

## Living the Greatest Commandments

- How does this passage help us love God?
- How does it help us love our neighbor?

## Prayer Focus and Weekly Challenge

Provide one focused prayer direction and one achievable act of obedience.

## Leader Reflection

- What challenged me?
- Where must I obey?
- What might my group struggle with?
- How can I shepherd them with truth and grace?

## Resource Connections

Link related articles, devotionals, studies, games, podcast episodes, teaching tools, maps, charts, or further reading when available.
