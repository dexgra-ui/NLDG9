Primary texts

Matthew • Mark • Luke • John

Accuracy note: The Gospels preserve real movements and locations, but they are not written as a modern day-by-day travel log. Some sequences differ by Gospel emphasis, and the exact order of several journeys cannot be established with certainty. NLDG therefore shows regions and well-attested movements rather than inventing one continuous route.

Places to know

Read the Gospels with the land in view.

Nazareth

Jesus’ hometown in Galilee and the setting for His early life and rejection in His hometown.

Matthew 2:23; Luke 4:16–30

Capernaum

A major base for Jesus’ Galilean ministry on the northwest shore of the Sea of Galilee.

Matthew 4:13; Mark 1–2

Cana

Galilean town associated with Jesus’ first sign in John and a later healing.

John 2:1–11; 4:46–54

Bethsaida

Associated with several disciples and Gospel events near the Sea of Galilee. The precise archaeological identification remains debated.

John 1:44; Luke 9:10

Caesarea Philippi

Northern setting for Peter’s confession that Jesus is the Messiah.

Matthew 16:13–20; Mark 8:27–30

Samaria / Sychar

Setting for Jesus’ conversation with the Samaritan woman and the witness that followed.

John 4:1–42

Jericho

Jordan Valley city associated with healing, Zacchaeus, and Jesus’ final approach toward Jerusalem.

Mark 10:46–52; Luke 19:1–10

Bethany

Village near Jerusalem associated with Martha, Mary, Lazarus, and events leading into Holy Week.

John 11–12

Jerusalem

The central city of the final Gospel week: Temple teaching, conflict, the Last Supper, arrest, trial, crucifixion, and resurrection appearances.

Matthew 21–28 and parallels

Why geography matters

Distance, terrain, and borders shape the story.

Jesus’ ministry crosses social and geographic boundaries. Galilee was not Jerusalem. Samaria carried deep historical tension. The Decapolis was heavily Gentile. Caesarea Philippi stood near prominent pagan worship. Seeing those settings helps readers notice why certain encounters, questions, and journeys carried particular weight.
