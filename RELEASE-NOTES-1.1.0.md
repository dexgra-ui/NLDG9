# No Labels, Designed by God — Version 1.1.0

Released: August 8, 2026

Version 1.1.0 strengthens the ministry platform, completes the Walking with Jesus collection, and improves the safeguards that keep the site dependable across devices.

## Walking with Jesus

- Completed all 21 self-paced lessons
- Added the full study collection to ministry discovery and navigation
- Added automated checks confirming that every lesson remains complete and reachable

## Site trust and accessibility

- Published the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use
- Improved legal links in the shared footer
- Refined accessibility across the ministry platform
- Strengthened Interactive Bible Study layout and quality checks
- Improved installable-app metadata and offline cache handling

## Quality assurance

- Expanded repository-completion checks
- Continued automated accessibility reporting
- Added Walking with Jesus audit coverage
- Verified responsive behavior and critical ministry routes

## Ministry platform carried forward

- Searchable studies, devotionals, articles, games, and ministry resources
- Personal library, saved progress, notes, and My Journey tools
- Leadership and discipleship resources
- Six expanded Bible games with team and presentation support
- Responsive and offline-ready experience

## Architecture boundary

Personal progress and notes remain stored on the visitor's device. Secure accounts, cross-device synchronization, online multiplayer, and cloud collaboration require a protected backend and are not represented as available in this release.
