New Believer Mentor Guide

Do not leave people to walk alone.

A complete companion guide for mentors, pastors, family members, and ministry leaders walking beside someone through the ten-step New Believers Path.

Relational discipleshipListen. Open Scripture. Walk together.

The mentor is not the answer. The mentor helps the learner encounter Jesus, understand Scripture, ask honest questions, and take faithful next steps.

The mentoring posture

Guide without controlling.

Healthy discipleship combines biblical clarity with patience, humility, safety, and genuine relationship.

Scripture first

Begin with the biblical text and distinguish it from interpretation, tradition, and personal opinion.

Listen carefully

Learn the person’s story, questions, fears, background, and pace before trying to correct everything.

Never manipulate

Do not pressure decisions, use shame, demand loyalty, or confuse strong emotion with spiritual maturity.

Know your limits

Pastoral care does not replace medical, mental-health, legal, recovery, safety, or emergency support.

Ten conversations

A mentor session for every step.

All ten sessions are available. Work through them in order, slow down when needed, and return to earlier conversations when new questions arise.

Before you mentor

Relationship comes before the lesson plan.

The guide provides structure, but the person in front of you matters more than finishing every question. Follow up consistently and make room for the learner to think, pray, heal, and grow.
