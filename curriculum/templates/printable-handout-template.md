# Printable Handout Template

Create a one- or two-page handout that remains useful without the full participant guide.

## Front

- Curriculum and lesson title
- Primary Scripture
- Big idea
- Three main truths
- Memory verse
- Space for notes

## Back

- Three discussion or reflection questions
- Living the Greatest Commandments
- Weekly challenge
- Prayer focus
- Related resource or next lesson
- Ministry website and contact information

## Print standards

- Design for US Letter paper.
- Use readable type at normal print scale.
- Preserve strong contrast in color and grayscale.
- Avoid background-heavy designs that waste ink.
- Keep important content inside safe print margins.
- Do not require QR codes to understand the handout.
- Verify URLs and QR codes before release.
