How the journey works

Scripture leads every stage.

Read the biblical account first. Where a visual scene is used, return to Scripture and distinguish what the Bible actually says from what a production imaginatively supplies.

Use it your way. Walk through all 29 lessons, begin directly with the 21-week core, use the four-lesson preparation section on its own, or use The Cross and the Empty Tomb for Holy Week and Easter.
