Accuracy First

A family tree is only as good as its labels.

This reference deliberately refuses to make Scripture appear more certain than it is. A stated parent is different from an inferred ancestor. A legal or household father is different from a biological claim. A name inside a parable is labeled as a parable character. Angelic beings are not inserted into human genealogy. Repeated names remain separate until the biblical text or strong cross-book evidence actually connects them.

Scripture-stated

A parent, spouse, child, sibling, relative, household, tribal, royal, priestly, or other connection is recorded as explicit only when the biblical text states it.

Probability stays labeled

Likely identities such as Nathanael/Bartholomew, Thaddaeus/Judas of James, Silas/Silvanus, John Mark/Mark, Cleopas/Clopas, and some repeated royal or priestly names remain marked probable when Scripture never directly equates them.

Variants stay visible

Matthew and Luke’s different recorded genealogies, manuscript spellings, compressed generations, alternate royal names, and textual questions such as Korah, Zechariah, Cainan, Heldai/Helem, and other difficult readings are preserved rather than silently harmonized.
