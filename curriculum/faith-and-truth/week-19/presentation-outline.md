# Teaching Presentation Outline
## AI and Human Uniqueness

1. **Title** — AI and Human Uniqueness
2. **Opening Question** — What daily responsibility would you automate?
3. **Primary Scripture** — Genesis 1:26–28
4. **Big Idea** — Dignity is received from God, not earned through ability.
5. **Image of God** — Relationship, representation, responsibility, creativity, and moral calling
6. **Worth Beyond Performance** — Infants, disability, illness, age, dependence, and dignity
7. **What AI Can Do** — Pattern recognition, generation, prediction, translation, organization
8. **What AI Cannot Replace** — Love, worship, moral accountability, embodied presence, discipleship
9. **Biblical Worldview** — Tool, stewardship, truth, responsibility, neighbor-love
10. **Discernment Questions** — Is it truthful? Who benefits? Who may be harmed? Who remains accountable?
11. **Case Study** — AI-assisted schoolwork, sermon preparation, or employment decision
12. **Greatest Commandments** — Love God and love neighbor in technology use
13. **Discussion** — What responsibility should never be handed to a machine?
14. **Weekly Challenge** — Honor an overlooked person and review one AI practice
15. **Prayer** — Wisdom without fear; innovation without pride; service without dehumanization
