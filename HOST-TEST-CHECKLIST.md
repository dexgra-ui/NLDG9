# Host Game Test Checklist

Use this checklist before Bible study, youth night, family game night, or a church presentation.

## Before people arrive

- Open the Game Center and confirm the page loads.
- Choose the correct audience: Preschool, Kids, Teens, Adults, or Family.
- Open the game being used.
- Set the correct number of teams and team names.
- Confirm the selected starting team.
- Test one correct answer and confirm points are added once.
- Test one incorrect answer and confirm no points are added.
- Test team rotation.
- Test +100, -100, Undo, Skip, and Reset Scores.
- Confirm the scoreboard fits the screen.
- Confirm sound and celebration effects are appropriate for the room.

## Presentation setup

- Connect the laptop or tablet to the TV or projector.
- Confirm the display is mirrored.
- Enter fullscreen mode.
- Check that questions and answer buttons are readable from the back of the room.
- Confirm the host can still reach all scoring controls.
- Turn off device notifications and automatic screen locking.
- Keep the device connected to power when possible.

## Game-specific checks

### Scripture or Suspicion, Who Am I, and Finish the Verse

- Answer buttons respond once per question.
- Correct-answer scoring occurs once.
- Next Question advances and changes teams properly.

### Lightning Round

- Timer starts and expires correctly.
- A timed-out question does not award points.
- The next question and team rotation work properly.

### Memory Match

- Cards open and close correctly.
- A match awards points once.
- The same team keeps the turn after a match.
- A missed match changes teams.

### Bible Jeopardy

- Clues open and reveal correctly.
- The correct clue value is awarded.
- No Award advances play without changing a score.
- Used clues remain unavailable.

## After the event

- Record any confusing question, scoring error, display issue, or slow control.
- Note the device, browser, game, audience, and number of teams involved.
- Reset recent-question history only when a fresh rotation is needed.
