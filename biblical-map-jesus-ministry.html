Location confidence

Secure or high-confidence sites use standard markers. Proposed or debated identifications use dashed gold markers and explanatory popups.

Accuracy note: The Gospels preserve real movements and locations, but they are not written as a modern day-by-day travel log. Some sequences differ by Gospel emphasis, and the exact order of several journeys cannot be established with certainty. NLDG therefore shows regions and well-attested movements rather than inventing one continuous route.

Why geography matters

Distance, terrain, and borders shape the story.

Jesus’ ministry crosses social and geographic boundaries. Galilee was not Jerusalem. Samaria carried deep historical tension. The Decapolis was heavily Gentile. Caesarea Philippi stood near prominent pagan worship. Seeing those settings helps readers notice why certain encounters, questions, and journeys carried particular weight.
