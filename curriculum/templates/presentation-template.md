# Teaching Presentation Template

Use widescreen 16:9 unless a ministry setting requires another format. Slides support the teacher; they do not replace the Bible or leader preparation.

## Required slides

1. **Title** — curriculum, lesson title, Scripture, ministry branding
2. **Big idea** — one clear sentence
3. **Opening question** — brief and readable
4. **Scripture focus** — reference and short excerpt when permitted
5. **Context** — map, timeline, setting, or background only when useful
6. **Teaching point one** — concise heading and key truth
7. **Teaching point two**
8. **Teaching point three**
9. **Discussion** — no more than three questions per slide
10. **Living the Greatest Commandments**
11. **Weekly challenge**
12. **Prayer focus**

## Optional slides

- Word study
- Biblical worldview comparison
- Current-culture example
- The Chosen companion note
- Chart, map, or timeline
- Difficult question
- Memory verse
- Related resource

## Design standards

- Use large text readable from the back of a room.
- Keep one main idea per slide.
- Avoid paragraphs and crowded bullet lists.
- Use approved ministry branding and high-contrast layouts.
- Do not use copyrighted artwork, television stills, or logos without permission.
- Add meaningful alternative text when the presentation format supports it.
- Keep Scripture references visible.
- Clearly label creative dramatization, interpretation, and direct biblical text.

## Speaker notes

For each teaching slide, include:

- Purpose
- Main explanation
- Transition
- Optional illustration
- Time guidance
- Question or application
