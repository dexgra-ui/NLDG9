How it works

Teach directly from the website.

1️⃣

Open a lesson

Choose a presentation-ready study and open the lesson you plan to teach.

2️⃣

Choose Teaching View

Teaching View organizes the lesson for leading a class, group, or ministry gathering.

3️⃣

Start Presentation

Use fullscreen mode on a TV or projector. Move through the slides with the on-screen controls, arrow keys, or spacebar.

📝

Use Speaker Notes

Keep private teaching reminders and transitions with you while the presentation is open.
