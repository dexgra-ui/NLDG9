A simple devotional rhythm

Read more slowly. Respond more faithfully.

A devotional is not about finishing quickly. It creates room for Scripture to shape the way we think, pray, and live.

1. ReadBegin with the Scripture reference.
2. ReflectNotice what challenges or encourages you.
3. RespondChoose one faithful action for today.
4. PraySpeak honestly with God.
