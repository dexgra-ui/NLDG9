Exploring Christianity?

You are welcome to begin before you have every answer.

Use this path to understand who Jesus is, what Christians mean by salvation, and what it looks like to trust Him. Honest questions are not a threat to faith. They can become part of the journey toward truth.

Recently trusted Christ?

New life needs a strong foundation.

This path will help you understand assurance, Scripture, prayer, the Holy Spirit, the church, baptism, temptation, sharing your faith, and daily discipleship.

How this path teaches

Scripture first. Interpretations labeled honestly.

Each lesson begins with the biblical text, explains what the passage clearly teaches, and then identifies areas where faithful Christians may understand secondary questions differently.

Read in context

We consider the surrounding passage, original audience, literary form, and wider biblical story.

Separate text and interpretation

Scripture is authoritative. Our explanations and traditions remain open to careful examination and correction.

Name Christian differences

Secondary disagreements are acknowledged without treating them as requirements for salvation.

Disclose our perspective

When Wesleyan-Holiness teaching shapes a lesson, that perspective is identified openly rather than presented as the only Christian wording.

The complete journey

Ten foundations for following Jesus.

Every step is available. Work through them in order or return to the area where you need the most help.

How to use this path

Move slowly enough for truth to take root.

This is not a test to pass. It is a guide for learning, reflecting, responding, and building healthy habits as you follow Jesus.

For each step

  1. Read the listed Scripture passages.
  2. Work through the teaching.
  3. Answer the reflection questions honestly.
  4. Write private notes saved on your device.
  5. Practice the suggested next step.
  6. Pray in your own words.
  7. Talk with a mature Christian or local church leader.
