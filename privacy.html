Your privacy matters

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information may be received through this website, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Last updated August 14, 2026

Who operates this website

No Labels, Designed by God is the public-facing ministry brand of No Labels Designed by God, LLC. In this policy, “the ministry,” “we,” and “us” refer to No Labels Designed by God, LLC.

Information you choose to share

When you contact the ministry, submit feedback, send a prayer request, or request information, we may receive details such as your name, email address, and the message you provide.

Please do not send passwords, financial account information, Social Security numbers, medical records, or other highly sensitive personal information.

Information stored on your device

Some study, game, profile, note, prayer, and progress features save information locally in your browser. Unless a feature clearly says otherwise, this local information remains on that device and is not sent to the ministry.

You can remove locally stored information through the relevant reset control or your browser settings.

Technical information

Our website host and other service providers may automatically process limited technical information—such as browser type, device type, requested pages, approximate location derived from an internet address, and error or security logs—to deliver, protect, and improve the site.

How information is used

  • To respond to questions, feedback, prayer requests, or ministry inquiries.
  • To maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the website and its resources.
  • To prevent misuse, fraud, or security threats.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by trusted providers that help operate email, hosting, forms, or other website services. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or with your permission.

A prayer request will not be intentionally shared beyond the ministry team unless you give permission, except when disclosure is reasonably necessary to address an immediate safety concern or legal obligation.

Retention and security

Information is kept only as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was received, ministry records, security, or legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, but no website, email system, or method of transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children's privacy

This website provides family and ministry resources, but it is not designed to collect personal information directly from children under 13. A parent or guardian should contact us if they believe a child has sent personal information so that we can review and, when appropriate, delete it.

External websites

Links to other websites, social platforms, video providers, or storefronts are governed by those services' own privacy practices. We encourage you to review their policies before providing information.

Your choices and questions

You may ask what personal information you have provided, request a correction or deletion when appropriate, or ask a privacy question by emailing team@nolabelsdesignedbygod.org. We may need to verify the request before acting on it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the ministry and website develop. The date shown at the top will identify the latest published version.
