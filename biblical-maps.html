Accuracy matters. Ancient geography is reconstructed from Scripture, archaeology, historical geography, and modern scholarship. Some locations, borders, and travel routes remain debated. NLDG maps identify uncertainty rather than presenting disputed reconstructions as certain.

Accuracy Key

How to read NLDG biblical maps.

Solid route

Well-attested or general movement supported by the biblical text.

Dashed route

Reconstructed, approximate, or debated route.

Shaded territory

Approximate ancient political or tribal region.

? Debated identification

A site or geographic identification for which substantial uncertainty remains.
