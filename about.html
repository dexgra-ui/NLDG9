Our Ministry

We are a family seeking to serve Christ together by creating biblical resources that help people discover who they are in Him.

Why we exist

Labels describe. God defines.

People are often reduced to their mistakes, backgrounds, struggles, politics, health, age, or other people’s expectations. We believe every person bears the image of God and should encounter biblical truth with dignity, clarity, grace, and hope.

No Labels, Designed by God exists to point people beyond the names the world gives them and toward the identity, purpose, and new life found in Jesus Christ.

No Labels, Designed by God is operated by No Labels Designed by God, LLC.

“We want to build something that points people to Jesus and can be carried by future generations.”

The people behind the ministry

Meet the Co-Founders.

No Labels, Designed by God is being built as a family ministry, combining biblical teaching, creative resources, and practical tools that can serve people now and future generations.

Co-Founder
Co-Founder & Ministry Lead

Dexter A. Graham

Dexter is a husband to Kenya, a father, Bible teacher, mentor, and ministry leader. He serves through Bible studies, men’s discipleship, mentoring, creative resources, and church media, always seeking practical ways to help people know Jesus and live out their faith.

His years of serving others, working in the technical field, and walking through dialysis have taught him that God meets people in ordinary responsibilities and difficult seasons. No Labels, Designed by God grew from his conviction that no mistake, struggle, diagnosis, background, or label gets the final word over a person’s life. Our truest identity and purpose are found in Jesus Christ.

“I do not have all the answers. I am someone learning to follow Jesus faithfully and helping others discover that their identity begins with the God who created them.”
Co-Founder
Co-Founder & Author

Kenya Graham

Kenya is a co-founder and author whose work helps shape the practical study resources within No Labels, Designed by God. Her Bible Study Companion approach gives readers space to slow down, observe Scripture, identify people, places, themes, and key words, ask questions, and record what they are learning.

Her work reflects the heart of the ministry: helping people engage God’s Word personally and grow in their understanding of Scripture. As No Labels continues to expand its publishing and discipleship resources, Kenya helps develop tools designed for individuals, families, and churches.
