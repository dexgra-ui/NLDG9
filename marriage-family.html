Marriage & Family

Rooted in Christ. Growing in love. Blessing others.

A Scripture-centered discipleship journey for marriages, parents, single adults, blended families, caregivers, and households seeking healthier communication, faithful relationships, practical wisdom, and a shared life shaped by Jesus.

Complete ten-study journeyHealthy homes are formed by grace and truth.

This path does not present one family structure, season, or personality as the standard for everyone. It helps households follow Jesus through love, honesty, responsibility, boundaries, repentance, belonging, and service.

“And over all these virtues put on love, which binds them all together in perfect unity.”

Colossians 3:14

The foundation

Family roles matter, but identity and worth begin with Christ.

Marriage and family life can carry joy, responsibility, grief, conflict, transition, and deep love. Some households include children. Others include a couple, one person, extended relatives, friends, caregivers, blended relationships, or people rebuilding after loss.

This journey teaches biblical convictions without idealizing one household shape or hiding harm. It makes room for singleness, marriage, parenting, infertility, divorce, widowhood, adoption, caregiving, blended families, and changing seasons.

How this journey teaches

Scripture, dignity, responsibility, and safety.

Every study joins biblical truth with practical discipleship and clear care for people in difficult or unsafe situations.

Scripture first

Family traditions and cultural expectations are examined in the light of the biblical text.

Grace and truth

Love tells the truth, receives forgiveness, accepts consequences, and pursues real change.

Every season matters

Marriage and parenthood are honored without treating them as the measure of adult worth.

Healthy authority

Leadership serves, listens, protects, accepts accountability, and refuses coercion.

Safety and care

Abuse, danger, trauma, and serious needs require qualified support and appropriate action.

Safety and care

Family unity must never be used to hide harm.

This journey supports marriage and family life while clearly rejecting violence, coercive control, child abuse, sexual coercion, stalking, financial abuse, spiritual intimidation, and forced reconciliation.

  • Immediate danger requires emergency and safety action.
  • Abuse disclosures require church policy, applicable law, and qualified support.
  • Forgiveness does not require silence, restored access, or remaining unsafe.
  • Children and vulnerable adults must be protected.
  • Pastoral care should work alongside legal, clinical, medical, and advocacy resources when needed.
