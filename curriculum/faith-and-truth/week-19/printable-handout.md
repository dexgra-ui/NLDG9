# AI and Human Uniqueness
## Faith & Truth in Today’s World · Week 19

**Scripture:** Genesis 1:26–28; Psalm 8:3–6; Psalm 139:13–18  
**Memory Verse:** Genesis 1:27

## Big Idea
Every human being has God-given dignity because humanity was created in the image of God. Our worth is not based on intelligence, productivity, health, independence, status, or usefulness.

## Three Truths to Remember
1. AI may imitate human abilities, but imitation does not create human dignity.
2. Technology is a tool under human stewardship; users remain responsible for truth and consequences.
3. Following Jesus requires us to use tools in ways that love God and honor our neighbors.

## Questions for Discernment
- Is this use truthful?
- Does it protect privacy and dignity?
- Who benefits, and who may be harmed?
- Am I hiding assistance or avoiding responsibility?
- Does this strengthen or replace meaningful human relationship?
- Does it help me love God and my neighbor?
