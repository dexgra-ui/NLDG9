Book by Book.

Explore Scripture in context, one biblical book at a time. Each collection includes background, passage study, teaching movements, discussion, personal examination, weekly practice, and prayer.

Understand each book on its own terms, then carry its truth into everyday life.

How the library works

Every book receives the space it needs.

Shorter biblical books may become focused series, while longer books may be divided into sections or volumes. Progress remains separate for each book, allowing you to study at your own pace.
