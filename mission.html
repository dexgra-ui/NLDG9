Why we exist

Our Mission

To help people move beyond the labels placed on them, discover their identity in Christ, grow through Scripture, and live out their faith in their families, churches, and communities.

Start Here Explore Bible Studies

The heart of the ministry

Labels may describe part of your story. They do not define your worth.

People are often reduced to their mistakes, backgrounds, struggles, politics, health, age, appearance, or other people’s expectations. Scripture tells a deeper story. Every person is created in the image of God, and new life, identity, purpose, and hope are found in Jesus Christ.

No Labels, Designed by God exists to point people toward that truth and provide practical resources that help them keep growing.

“For we are God’s handiwork, created in Christ Jesus to do good works.”

Ephesians 2:10

What we believe

The convictions that guide what we create.

🫶

Every person bears God’s image

Human dignity does not come from status, success, popularity, health, or the approval of others. It begins with being created by God.

✝️

Identity and new life are found in Christ

Jesus does more than improve our image. He forgives, restores, transforms, and calls us into a new way of living.

📖

Scripture is our foundation

The Bible guides our teaching, our values, and the way we respond to the questions people face today.

🤝

Truth and grace belong together

We do not have to choose between biblical conviction and genuine compassion. Jesus calls us to both.

🏠

Discipleship is lived out

Faith should shape our homes, relationships, work, decisions, service, and the way we treat people.

🌱

Growth happens in community

These resources are designed to support personal growth, families, small groups, and local churches rather than replace them.

Who we serve

People at every stage of the journey.

This ministry is for people who are curious about faith, new believers learning the basics, Christians seeking deeper growth, families building a stronger spiritual foundation, and church leaders looking for practical tools.

It is especially for anyone who has felt overlooked, judged, reduced to a label, or unsure whether there is still a place for them in God’s story.
