New Believer Mentor Guide

Do not leave people to walk alone.

A companion guide for mentors, pastors, family members, and ministry leaders walking beside someone through the ten-step New Believers Path.

Begin Session 1View Learner Path
Relational discipleshipListen. Open Scripture. Walk together.

The mentor is not the answer. The mentor helps the learner encounter Jesus, understand Scripture, ask honest questions, and take faithful next steps.

The mentoring posture

Guide without controlling.

Healthy discipleship combines biblical clarity with patience, humility, safety, and genuine relationship.

Scripture first

Begin with the biblical text and distinguish it from interpretation, tradition, and personal opinion.

Listen carefully

Learn the person’s story, questions, fears, background, and pace before trying to correct everything.

Never manipulate

Do not pressure decisions, use shame, demand loyalty, or confuse strong emotion with spiritual maturity.

Know your limits

Pastoral care does not replace medical, mental-health, legal, recovery, or emergency support.

Ten conversations

A mentor session for every step.

Session 1 is ready. The remaining sessions will follow the same Scripture-centered and practical structure.

Before you mentor

Relationship comes before the lesson plan.

The guide provides structure, but the person in front of you matters more than finishing every question. Slow down when needed, follow up consistently, and make room for the learner to think, pray, and grow.

Every session will include

Preparation, central Scripture, a suggested meeting flow, conversation questions, common misunderstandings, practical next steps, prayer, follow-up, safety boundaries, private notes, and a mentor checklist.
