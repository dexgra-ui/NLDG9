Who God Says You Are

Hello, friend—

The world is quick to place labels on us. Sometimes we even begin repeating those labels to ourselves. But none of those things have the final word. God does.

“For we are God’s workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared ahead of time for us to do.”
Ephesians 2:10

Your life is not an accident. God did not make a mistake when He made you. Jesus invites us to receive our identity from our Creator and then live from that identity.
