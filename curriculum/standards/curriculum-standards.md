# Curriculum Standards

## Biblical Faithfulness

- Interpret each passage in context.
- Distinguish clear biblical teaching from inference, tradition, and opinion.
- Use supporting passages responsibly.
- Avoid forcing Scripture to answer questions it does not directly address.
- Keep Jesus and the gospel central.

## Theological Clarity

- Explain doctrine in accessible language.
- Define technical terms when they are necessary.
- Identify major Christian convictions and acknowledge meaningful differences when appropriate.
- Do not use uncertainty as an excuse for vagueness.

## Practical Discipleship

Every lesson must include one clear response for personal life and should consider family, church, work, community, and mission when relevant.

## Human Dignity and Safety

- Treat every person as created in the image of God.
- Avoid stereotypes, shame, manipulation, or dehumanizing language.
- Include safeguarding guidance when lessons involve abuse, self-harm, exploitation, children, vulnerable adults, or criminal conduct.
- Encourage qualified pastoral, medical, legal, or mental-health support when needed.

## Discussion Quality

Questions should progress through:

1. Observation — What does the text say?
2. Interpretation — What does it mean?
3. Application — How should it change us?
4. Mission — How should it affect the way we love and serve others?

## Curriculum Package Requirements

A complete lesson includes:

- Lesson snapshot and big idea
- Learning objectives
- Primary and supporting Scripture
- Participant guide
- Expanded leader guide
- Teaching outline
- Discussion coaching
- Prayer focus
- Weekly challenge
- Memory verse when appropriate
- Resource connections
- Print-ready structure

## Quality Review

Before publication, confirm:

- The lesson is biblically responsible.
- The central idea is clear.
- The participant can identify a faithful next step.
- A newer leader can prepare and teach confidently.
- Difficult questions are handled honestly.
- The tone reflects truth and grace.
- The content aligns with the mission of No Labels, Designed by God.
