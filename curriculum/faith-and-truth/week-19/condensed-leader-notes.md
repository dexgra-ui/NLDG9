# Condensed Leader Notes
## AI and Human Uniqueness

**Big Idea:** Human dignity comes from God’s image, not intelligence, productivity, health, status, or usefulness.

**Primary Text:** Genesis 1:26–28

## Five Teaching Movements
1. God gives humanity identity before assigning responsibility.
2. Human worth remains intact across age, disability, illness, dependence, and changing ability.
3. AI can imitate useful abilities without becoming morally accountable, spiritually alive, or relationally present.
4. Tools extend human intentions, systems, and power; users remain responsible.
5. Christian technology use must serve truth, stewardship, human dignity, and neighbor-love.

## Key Cautions
- Do not reduce the image of God to intelligence.
- Do not present debated claims about AI consciousness as settled fact.
- Do not let the conversation become a technical competition.
- Do not shame participants for either enthusiasm or concern.
- Keep returning to Scripture, responsibility, and love of neighbor.

## Best Discussion Questions
- Why is productivity a dangerous measure of worth?
- What responsibility should never be handed to a machine?
- Where can AI serve people, and where can it weaken human dignity?
- What faithful boundary or practice should we adopt this week?

## Closing Challenge
Honor one overlooked person and evaluate one use of AI for truthfulness, responsibility, privacy, and human impact.
