Interactive Toolkit

TOOL 1

Decision & Discernment Guide

Related: Leadership Studies 4 and 8. Make the decision in the light, not in isolation.

Decision or question
Responsible person, board, or team
Mission and Scripture alignment
Facts currently known
Unknowns and assumptions to verify
Affected people and voices to hear
Wise counsel and qualified expertise needed
Realistic options
Risks, costs, unintended harm, and safeguards
Decision, communication plan, and next step
Review date

A careful process does not guarantee an easy outcome. It helps reduce secrecy, haste, and personality-driven decisions.

TOOL 2

Team Covenant Builder

Related: Leadership Studies 3 and 6. Build this agreement together and review it regularly.

Team or ministry name
Shared mission: why we exist and who we serve
Values we will practice
Roles, decisions, and authority
Communication and meeting rhythm
How we address disagreement and correction
Confidentiality and its limits
Safeguarding and reporting commitments
Accountability, feedback, workload, and rest
How we repair trust when we fall short
Review date

A covenant must never be used to silence questions, hide misconduct, or demand loyalty to a leader over truth and safety.

TOOL 3

Accountability & Safeguarding Plan

Related: Leadership Studies 2 and 8. Good intentions are not a substitute for strong systems.

Role, ministry, or area covered
Meaningful oversight and review rhythm
Financial controls and separation of duties
Decision records and appropriate access to information
Safe concern and complaint channels
Protection against retaliation
Conflicts of interest and recusal process
Relational, counseling, digital, and sexual boundaries
Child, youth, vulnerable-adult, and volunteer safeguards
When external authorities or professionals are contacted
Emergency response and immediate protection steps
Review date

Follow applicable law, denominational policy, insurer requirements, employment rules, and qualified professional guidance.

TOOL 4

Conflict Repair Worksheet

Related: Leadership Studies 7 and 8. Seek truth and repair without confusing reconciliation with unsafe access.

Safety boundary: Do not use an internal worksheet to privately manage abuse, credible threats, harassment, exploitation, criminal conduct, or serious safeguarding concerns. Protect people and report appropriately.
Issue to address without insults or diagnosing motives
Facts that can be verified
Interpretations or assumptions to hold carefully
People affected and impact described
What each person needs to be heard
Responsibility to own and truth to speak
Repair, restitution, or changed behavior needed
Boundaries, separation, or role changes needed
Reporting, mediation, or qualified support needed
Follow-up responsibility, measurement, and date

Forgiveness does not automatically restore trust, access, employment, volunteer service, or leadership authority.

TOOL 5

Leader Development Plan

Related: Leadership Studies 6 and 10. Develop people through formation, opportunity, feedback, and release.

Developing leader or role identifier
Character and spiritual formation goals
Strengths and gifts already visible
Skills or knowledge to develop
Meaningful opportunity to practice
Mentoring, training, and resources provided
Feedback rhythm and who gives it
Authority granted and current limits
Safeguards and support during mistakes
Milestones and plan for expanding responsibility
What the current leader must stop controlling or carrying

Respect calling, pace, accessibility, family responsibilities, employment, health, and the freedom to say no.

TOOL 6

Leadership Transition Plan

Related: Leadership Studies 9 and 10. Transfer responsibility without abandoning people or preserving control.

Role or ministry transitioning and target handoff date
Reason and goals for the transition
Responsibilities that must be transferred
Documents, calendars, processes, and history
Relationships requiring thoughtful introduction
Accounts, keys, equipment, data, and permissions
Financial, legal, policy, and safeguarding handoff
Successor selection or interim coverage
Training, shadowing, and decision-transfer schedule
Communication to team, church, partners, and community
Risks, unfinished matters, and support needed
Outgoing leader’s role and boundaries after handoff
Care for outgoing and incoming leaders
What must be true before the transition is complete?

A faithful transition serves the ministry and people rather than protecting one person’s status or forcing a successor to imitate the past.
