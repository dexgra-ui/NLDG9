Packet overview

New Believer Discipleship Ministry Packet

This packet supports the New Believers Path and Mentor Guide. It helps a church, family, or ministry establish expectations, match people carefully, track meetings, review progress, and continue walking with the learner after the ten sessions.

Use of this packet

  • Keep records secure and collect only information the ministry truly needs.
  • Do not store sensitive details on shared devices or in publicly accessible files.
  • Follow the church’s safeguarding policy and applicable law.
  • Mentoring does not replace professional medical, mental-health, recovery, legal, or emergency services.
  • The purpose is relational discipleship, not merely completing forms.

Safety, Confidentiality, and Referral Guide

Immediate danger comes before completing a lesson.

When someone may be in immediate danger, contact appropriate emergency services or qualified local help. Follow the church’s safeguarding policy and applicable law. Do not attempt to investigate abuse or handle a crisis beyond your training.

Explain confidentiality honestly

  • Do not promise absolute secrecy.
  • Share private information only with those who need it for responsible care, safety, supervision, or required reporting.
  • Tell the learner when information must be shared, unless doing so would increase immediate danger or violate professional guidance.
  • Keep records secure and factual. Avoid unnecessary sensitive detail.
