Before you share

Prayer care with dignity and privacy.

Private means private from the wall.

Private requests are sent to the ministry prayer-care inbox and are not intentionally published.

Public means reviewed first.

Choosing public gives permission for an approved, privacy-conscious version of your request to appear on the Prayer Wall.

Protect identifying details.

Do not include addresses, phone numbers, financial information, medical records, full names of other people, or other sensitive details.

This is not an emergency service.

If you or someone else may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a qualified local professional now.
