# Leadership Quality Review

This review validates the completed Leadership journey without changing published ministry content.

## Automated coverage

- Leadership Center availability and ten study cards
- Ten ordered studies and complete previous/next sequence
- Study rendering, reflection fields, completion message, and print controls
- Favorite, completion, progress, and private-note persistence after reload
- Study-library search integration
- Mobile, tablet, laptop, and desktop overflow checks
- WCAG 2 A/AA and 2.1 A/AA checks on the collection and representative studies
- Printable PDF generation for Studies 1 and 10

## Review status

The focused audit runs through `.github/workflows/leadership-quality-audit.yml`. Its Markdown report and sample PDFs are uploaded as the `leadership-quality-audit` workflow artifact.
