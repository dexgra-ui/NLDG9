How these studies teach

Children deserve real Bible study, not watered-down adult material.

Growing with Jesus speaks directly to children in clear language while keeping Scripture, context, grace, truth, dignity, and careful application at the center.

Scripture first

Children are invited to open a Bible, read the passage, notice what it says, and ask honest questions.

Clear, not childish

Ideas are explained simply without pretending children cannot think deeply about God, character, relationships, and faith.

Grace and truth

Mistakes are taken seriously without using shame, fear, comparison, or spiritual performance to motivate children.

Safe participation

No account, public profile, comments, or personal-information submission is needed to use these studies.

Child safety and dignity

Spiritual formation should never require a child to overshare.

  • These studies do not ask children to submit names, ages, schools, locations, photos, contact details, or private prayer information.
  • Adults should not pressure children to disclose painful experiences in front of a group.
  • If a child shares that someone is hurting them, they are unsafe, or they may hurt themselves or someone else, follow your organization’s safeguarding procedures and applicable reporting requirements rather than promising secrecy.
  • Questions about disability, family structure, learning differences, appearance, grades, friendships, or behavior should be handled without shame or comparison.
