# Condensed Leader Notes Template

Use this two-page maximum guide for experienced teachers or last-minute review. It supplements rather than replaces the expanded leader guide.

## Page one

- Lesson title and Scripture
- Big idea
- Main objective
- Three teaching points
- Essential context
- Key theological truth
- One common misunderstanding
- Suggested timing

## Page two

- Discussion questions and follow-ups
- Difficult-question response
- Safety or pastoral-care note, when relevant
- Weekly challenge
- Prayer focus
- Greatest Commandments application
- Next lesson or related resource

## Standard

A leader should be able to glance at this document during the session without reading paragraphs aloud. Use phrases, transitions, and concise prompts rather than a full manuscript.
