# No Labels, Designed by God — Version 1.0.0

Release date: August 6, 2026

Version 1.0 establishes the production-ready ministry platform at [nolabelsdesignedbygod.org](https://nolabelsdesignedbygod.org).

## Highlights

- A revised responsive homepage with clear paths into Bible studies, ministry resources, and Bible games
- Participant, Leader, Teaching, Presentation, and Print experiences for published studies
- Universal baseline Leader Mode coverage for every published individual lesson, while preserving expanded custom guides
- Teaching Dashboard, Teaching Notebook, Scripture Study Panel, Resource Drawer, Discussion Manager, Print Center, and Teaching Analytics
- Church-ready Bible games, including Scripture or Suspicion, Bible Jeopardy, Family Feud-style play, and Faith Wheel
- Search, My Library, My Journey, responsive layouts, keyboard accessibility, reduced-motion support, and offline caching
- Automated site-quality, SEO/social-preview, accessibility, leadership-quality, game-pack, Leader Mode coverage, and repository-completion audits
