The foundation

Scripture leads every lesson.

The visual scene is a discussion tool, not a replacement for the Bible. Every lesson identifies the biblical connection, creative additions, precise start and stop cues, and a Scripture check.

Use the study at your own pace. Follow the complete lesson flow or select the sections that serve your personal study, family, class, or group. No full episode is required.
