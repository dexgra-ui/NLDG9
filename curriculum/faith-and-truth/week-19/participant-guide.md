# Participant Guide
## AI and Human Uniqueness

### Scripture
Genesis 1:26–28; Psalm 8:3–6; Psalm 139:13–18; Colossians 1:15–17

### Big Idea
Our worth is not based on what we can produce or how intelligent we appear. Every human being has God-given dignity because humanity was created in the image of God.

### Opening Question
If technology could complete one daily responsibility perfectly, which responsibility would you choose, and what would you do with the time it saved?

### Learn
1. Human dignity is received from God.
2. Worth is not measured by intelligence, productivity, health, or independence.
3. AI can imitate abilities without becoming a bearer of God’s image.
4. Technology remains a tool under human stewardship.
5. Christians remain responsible for truth, attribution, privacy, relationships, and consequences.

### Discuss
1. What stands out in Genesis 1:26–28?
2. Why is it dangerous to measure worth by productivity?
3. Which abilities can AI imitate well?
4. Where can AI genuinely serve people?
5. Where can it weaken truth, privacy, responsibility, or relationships?
6. How can Christians avoid treating AI as either a savior or an enemy?
7. What responsibility should never be handed to a machine?
8. How will you honor another person’s dignity this week?

### Living the Greatest Commandments
Love God by refusing to make technology an idol or substitute for wisdom and prayer. Love your neighbor by protecting dignity, truth, privacy, attribution, and meaningful human care.

### Weekly Challenge
Notice five overlooked people and identify one way each reflects God-given dignity apart from status or productivity. Encourage at least one person. Review one use of AI and ask whether it is truthful, responsible, and honoring to people.

### Prayer
Creator God, thank You for making every person in Your image. Give us wisdom to use technology truthfully and responsibly. Protect us from fear, pride, deception, and idolatry. Teach us to love You fully and honor our neighbors. Amen.
