The foundation

Leadership is stewardship, not ownership.

God entrusts leaders with people, truth, resources, relationships, opportunities, and influence. None of these belong to the leader.

This journey places character before charisma, service before status, accountability before independence, and multiplication before personal control.

Leadership safeguards

Spiritual authority must never be used to control, exploit, or silence.

This journey directly addresses domination, secrecy, favoritism, financial misconduct, sexual misconduct, retaliation, spiritual intimidation, unsafe counseling, and the misuse of confidentiality.

  • Leaders remain accountable to Christ, Scripture, policy, and appropriate oversight.
  • Abuse and serious misconduct require protection, reporting, and qualified review.
  • Forgiveness does not automatically restore a person to leadership.
  • Confidentiality must never hide danger, crime, exploitation, or safeguarding concerns.
  • Medical, legal, financial, and mental-health matters require qualified expertise.
