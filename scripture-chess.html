Wisdom & Strategy:
Scripture Chess

Think ahead. Know the Word.

Play real chess solo against the computer or with another player or team. Significant moves open Scripture challenges. Chess results and Scripture Points stay separate, so the board tests strategy while the questions build biblical understanding.

You play White. The computer plays Black and never earns Scripture Points. If it puts you in check, you receive a Stand Firm Scripture challenge.

CaptureAnswer a Scripture question.
CheckFace a context challenge.
CastleExplore meaning and wisdom.
PromoteTake a bonus challenge.
CheckmateFinish with the Big Question.

Game rule: Bible answers never change whether a chess move is legal. The legal chess move happens first; the Scripture challenge follows and can earn a separate Scripture Point.
