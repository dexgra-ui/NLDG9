## Welcome

Introduce the lesson in plain language. Explain why the subject matters for following Jesus today without assuming prior knowledge.

## Opening prayer

Provide a brief prayer inviting the Holy Spirit to guide understanding and obedience.

## Opening conversation

Include one accessible icebreaker or entry question connected to the lesson’s theme.

## Read the Scripture

List the primary passage and clear reading instructions. Encourage participants to notice repeated words, contrasts, commands, promises, and descriptions of God or people.

## Understand the passage

Divide the passage into logical sections. For each section include:

- What happens?
- What does it reveal about God or Jesus?
- What does it reveal about people?
- Why does it matter?

## Biblical truth

State the lesson’s central biblical truth clearly. Distinguish what Scripture directly teaches from reasonable interpretation or present-day application.

## Discussion

### Observe

Questions answered directly from the passage.

### Understand

Questions that explore meaning, context, and theology.

### Apply

Questions that connect truth to choices, relationships, habits, work, church, and community.

### Go

One question that moves the group toward witness, service, reconciliation, justice, compassion, or disciple-making.

## Living the Greatest Commandments

- How does this lesson help us love God more faithfully?
- How does this lesson help us love our neighbor more faithfully?

## Weekly challenge

Give one specific, realistic act of obedience. Avoid assigning many unrelated tasks.

## Personal reflection

Provide space for:

- What God is showing me
- What I need to obey
- Who I need to encourage or serve
- What I want to pray about

## Closing prayer

Connect the prayer to the lesson’s truth and weekly challenge.

## Continue growing

List only directly relevant articles, devotionals, studies, Scripture passages, games, or ministry tools.
